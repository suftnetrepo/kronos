Privacy Policy for Kronos

Effective Date: 7 May 2026

Kronos respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Kronos mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Kronos is designed to minimise data collection. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, or contact details.

Timetable, subject, homework, exam, reminder, and preference data are stored locally on your device.

2. Premium Subscriptions

Kronos offers optional premium features through Apple In-App Purchases. Payments and subscription management are handled securely by Apple.

We do not collect or store your payment card details.

3. Notifications

Kronos may request permission to send notifications for reminders. Notifications are used only to help you remember timetable, homework, or exam-related tasks.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

5. Data Storage

Your app data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, locally stored app data may also be removed.

6. Children’s Privacy

Kronos is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be made available through the app or the App Store listing.
